*Translated from the Chinese original. It was first posted on the 郁闷小屋 board of the CC98 forum on 21 September 2026.*

I feel as if my mental age has somehow arrived at the point where I no longer dare to enter a relationship between people from different social and family backgrounds.

I have had only one relationship, and I have not been in university for very long. Yet I clearly feel an inexplicable inferiority complex rise inside me whenever I face someone I am attracted to.

This feeling does not come from my appearance. It comes from the poverty of my hometown.

I can make sure that I become prosperous myself, but I have no way to make my hometown prosperous. If I enter a relationship, one day we will have to go home. By then, the journey across mountains and seas will surely wear down our patience, exhaust us, and leave us with a relationship that cannot even be called complete.

I am not judging a generous heart by a petty one. I believe that if I fell in love with someone from far away, I definitely would not care about this. I also believe that if my partner truly loved me, she would do the same.

But it is still a burden that two people would have to carry. It would be irresponsible to begin a relationship hastily before thinking this through. I have seen too many examples like this around me, and I am afraid of becoming one of them.

I want so badly to release my feelings. I want so badly to love boldly… Why have you become so insecure?
